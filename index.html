Research

My current research explores natural spaces of functions on quiver representations, in particular the free algebroid of a quiver and its functional calculus.

Given a polynomial with complex coefficients

$f(x) = \sum_i^na_ix^i$ <\div>, this can be evaluated on a square matrix $X$, simply by taking $f(X) = \sum_i^n a_iX^i$. Similarly, by applying a continuous function $f$ to the diagonal entries of a diagonal matrix, you can define $f(A)$ for any diagonalizable matrix $A$ by
$$f(A) = f(U^*\begin{pmatrix}a&\\&b\end{pmatrix}U) = U^*\begin{pmatrix} f(a) &\\&f(b) U$$
where $A = U^*DU$ is the spectral decomposition of $A$.
