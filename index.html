About Me

I am a doctoral candidate in the Department of Mathematics at Drexel University advised by James E. Pascoe. My work focuses on operator theory, particularly the free functional calculus, though my interests extend broadly across noncommutative algebra, noncommutative real algebraic geometry, as well as foundations of mathematics.

Research

My primary research is in operator theory, in particular the free functional calculus. I am particularly interested in its interplay with other fields, such as neural networks and logic. My interests also include noncommutative real algebraic geometry, noncommutative algebra, and category theory.
